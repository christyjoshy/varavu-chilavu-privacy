Varavu Chilavu: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Varavu Chilavu is a personal project made by Christy Joshy, an individual developer in India. Contact: christyjoshy316@gmail.com.

The short version. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There is no account on our servers, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We cannot see your data, and cannot recover it for you.

What the app stores, and where

On your device only:

  • In the app's database: your profile (a display name you choose, your currency, whether Face ID or fingerprint is on, and a one-way hash of your 4-digit PIN), accounts and balances, entries (amount, date, note, category, tags), transfers, budgets, savings goals, dues and EMIs, and the alerts the app has raised.
  • In the secure store (iOS Keychain; Android Keystore-backed storage): a marker for who is signed in on this device, and small flags such as "tour already shown" and your widget choices.
  • For the widget: a small summary (month, totals, top category, progress) that the app copies where the widget can read it, on the same device.

We do not receive any of it, and the app does not send it anywhere.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Face ID, fingerprint or face unlock: to unlock the app. Handled by your phone's operating system; the app never receives your biometric data.
  • Notifications: reminders and alerts that the app creates on your device (for example "rent due today" or a spending notice). They can include labels and amounts and may show on your lock screen, depending on your phone's settings. No push messages come from a server.

The app does not use your contacts, photos, camera, location or microphone.

Widgets

The home-screen widget shows this month's figures. It is visible whenever your home screen is, even while the app is locked. In the widget settings you can hide amounts.

What we do not do

No analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising identifiers, no tracking, no sale of data, no sharing with third parties, no over-the-air code updates.

Network use

The app does not make network requests of its own and works fully offline. It does not connect to your bank.

Not in this version: bank-statement import, Gmail sync and automatic sorting rules are switched off. If they are added later, this policy will be updated before they ship.

Backups and a new phone

We run no backup. Your phone's own backup may include the app's data:

  • iPhone: an iCloud or Finder backup includes the app's data. Restore a new phone from it and your data comes back. Delete the app, or lose the phone without a backup, and it is gone.
  • Android: Google's automatic app backup may copy the app's database to your Google account if you have Google backup on. After a restore you unlock with your PIN. Secure-store items (such as "who is signed in") are not restored.

The app has no export or "delete everything" button yet. Deleting the app removes the database.

Security

Your data is protected by your phone's own encryption and by the app's PIN or biometric lock (it locks again after about a minute in the background). The PIN is stored only as a one-way hash, and the database itself is not separately encrypted. No system is perfectly secure: someone with your unlocked phone, or a jailbroken or rooted phone, may be able to read app data.

Your rights and children

There is nothing for us to access, correct or delete: change or delete entries inside the app, or delete the app. The app is not directed at children under 13.
